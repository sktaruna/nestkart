Mission

NestKart exists to make it easier to find home goods worth keeping. The internet has made shopping easier but product discovery harder — too many choices, too many mediocre options, too little honest curation. NestKart is the alternative: fewer products, chosen deliberately.

Vision

A home where every object has been chosen, not just purchased. NestKart is building a curated home goods destination where customers can trust that every product listing has passed a real quality bar — not just a paid placement.

Our story

NestKart was founded in 2019 by two former interior designers who kept encountering the same problem: clients wanted to furnish and decorate their homes well, but couldn't find a single place that brought together quality independent makers and accessible price points.

The founding team spent a year sourcing directly from makers at craft fairs, studio visits, and design trade shows across the US — vetting materials, testing products, and building relationships with makers whose work would otherwise stay invisible online.

Team

NestKart is a 45-person company headquartered in Austin, Texas. The team includes buyers, logistics coordinators, customer experience specialists, and a small in-house photography studio.

🛒

Buying & Curation

NestKart's buying team attends trade shows, visits maker studios, and evaluates hundreds of products each quarter to decide what earns a listing.

📦

Logistics & Operations

Our logistics coordinators manage carrier relationships, warehouse operations, and the dispatch of every order to ensure the shipping commitments on the website hold.

💬

Customer Experience

The customer experience team handles support, returns, and disputes. Our target is a response within 1 business day on every email to support@nestkart.com.

📷

In-house Photography

All NestKart product photos are shot in-house under controlled lighting so colour and scale representation is as accurate as possible across devices.

Values

  • 1
    Curation over volume
    NestKart would rather list 500 great products than 5,000 average ones. Every listing is a deliberate choice, not a marketplace default.
  • 2
    Honest descriptions
    Product pages describe what an item is, not how it should make you feel. Dimensions, materials, and care instructions are accurate and verified.
  • 3
    Maker relationships
    NestKart works directly with makers wherever possible. We do not use dropshipping intermediaries for our core range — relationships with makers are how we hold quality accountable.
  • 4
    Transparent pricing
    The price shown on a product page includes applicable tax. There are no surprise fees at checkout beyond shipping, which is calculated and shown before you commit.
  • 5
    Accountable service
    If something goes wrong with an order, NestKart resolves it without requiring customers to fight for it. Damaged items, lost parcels, and incorrect orders are handled promptly and without argument.
