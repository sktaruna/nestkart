Every brand earns its place on NestKart.

Before listing, all brands pass material inspection, safety compliance checks, and a sample review by NestKart's in-house team. Brands must also meet NestKart's ethical sourcing code, which prohibits child labour and requires fair wages for all production workers.

Brands FAQ

More questions? Email hello@nestkart.com.

Some brand partners sell directly through their own websites. However, NestKart may offer exclusive colourways or bundle pricing not available elsewhere. Purchases made outside NestKart are not covered by NestKart's return policy.
Every brand goes through a product review process that includes material inspection, safety compliance checks, and a sample review by NestKart's in-house team. Brands must also meet NestKart's ethical sourcing code, which prohibits child labour and requires fair wages for all production workers.
Made-to-order furniture is produced after your purchase. These items are not held in stock and cannot be cancelled after 24 hours of ordering. Lead times are shown on individual product pages and are typically 4–6 weeks.
NestKart requires all brand partners to disclose material sourcing. Timber products must use FSC-certified wood. We are working toward full supply chain transparency by 2026. A sourcing disclosure is shown on each product page.
In the event of a product recall, NestKart will contact all affected customers by email within 24 hours and process full refunds automatically, including return shipping at NestKart's cost.
