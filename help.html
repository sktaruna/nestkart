📦 Orders

Browse the Shop page, click Add to Cart on any product, and proceed to checkout. You can check out as a guest or with a NestKart account. NestKart accounts allow you to track orders, save addresses, and view order history.
Orders can be cancelled within 2 hours of placement if they have not yet been dispatched. Email support@nestkart.com with your order number immediately. After 2 hours or dispatch (whichever is sooner), cancellation is not possible — you can return the item on delivery.
You will receive a dispatch email with a tracking link when your order leaves our warehouse. Tracking updates within 24 hours of dispatch. You can also view tracking from My Account → Orders.
If your order has not arrived within 7 business days of the estimated delivery date, email support@nestkart.com with your order number. NestKart will investigate with the carrier and either reship or issue a full refund within 3 business days.
Order modifications are not possible after placement. If you want to add or swap an item, cancel the order within 2 hours (if not yet dispatched) and place a new order.

↩️ Returns & Refunds

Email support@nestkart.com with your order number and reason for return. NestKart will respond within 1 business day with return instructions. The standard return window is 30 days from delivery (14 days for furniture over $300).
Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days of NestKart receiving your return. Your bank may take a further 2–5 business days to post the credit. Total time from return dispatch to credit is typically 8–14 business days.
NestKart does not offer direct exchanges. Return the item for a refund and place a new order for the replacement item.
Email support@nestkart.com with your order number and a photo of the item received. NestKart will arrange collection of the incorrect item and dispatch the correct item within 2 business days, at no cost to you.
Original shipping costs are refunded only if the return is due to a defective, damaged, or incorrect item. Change-of-mind returns do not include a refund of original shipping fees.

🛋️ Products

NestKart photographs products under controlled lighting to ensure accuracy. However, screen calibration varies between devices. Terracotta, natural linen, and wood-finish items in particular may appear slightly lighter or darker in person.
It depends on the product. Standard range items are restocked regularly — join the waitlist on the product page to be notified. Seasonal and limited-run items may not be restocked.
All product dimensions on NestKart are listed in centimetres (cm). 1 inch = 2.54cm. If you need measurements in inches, email support@nestkart.com.
You can submit a product suggestion at hello@nestkart.com. NestKart reviews suggestions quarterly when curating new brands and items. We cannot guarantee listings based on requests.
Most products are in stock and dispatch within 1–3 business days. Made-to-order items are clearly labelled on the product page with their lead time (typically 4–6 weeks).
