Alaska & Hawaii surcharges

Standard shipping: Additional $12.00 surcharge for AK and HI orders.
Express shipping: Not available for Alaska or Hawaii.
Large item delivery: Additional $75.00 surcharge for AK and HI orders.

Dispatch times by product category

  • Décor and kitchenware Dispatched within 1 business day of order
  • Furniture (in stock) Dispatched within 2–3 business days of order
  • Furniture (made to order) 4–6 weeks lead time; stated clearly on the product page
  • Pre-order items Estimated dispatch date stated on the product page at time of purchase

Order tracking

After your order is dispatched, you will receive an email with a tracking link. Tracking updates within 24 hours of dispatch. If you do not receive a tracking email within 2 business days of your order, contact support@nestkart.com.

Return eligibility

Standard return window: 30 days from delivery date.

Eligible for return

  • Standard décor and kitchenware items (unused, 30 days)
  • Furniture items under $300 (unused, flat-pack not assembled, 30 days)
  • Furniture items over $300 (unused, flat-pack not assembled, 14-day window)
  • Defective or damaged items (free return shipping)

Not eligible for return

  • Made-to-order items (unless defective)
  • Personalised items (unless defective)
  • Opened candles and perishable items
  • Assembled flat-pack furniture

Condition requirements for return

  • Items must be unused and in original packaging
  • Tags must be attached for all textile items
  • Flat-pack furniture must not have been assembled

How to return an item

  1. 1
    Email support@nestkart.com with your order number and reason for return.
  2. 2
    NestKart responds within 1 business day with return authorisation and a prepaid or self-pay return label (depending on reason).
  3. 3
    Pack the item securely in its original packaging. Attach tags to textiles.
  4. 4
    Drop the parcel at the specified carrier location listed on your return label.
  5. 5
    Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days of NestKart receiving the return.

Return shipping costs

Defective, damaged, or incorrect items: Free return shipping. NestKart provides a prepaid label.

Change of mind: Customer pays return shipping. Estimated costs: $8–$15 for small items; $30–$60 for furniture.
