NestKart Buyer Guarantee

Every order placed on NestKart is covered by the following three guarantees:

Accurate descriptions

If an item significantly differs from its product description, NestKart will offer a full refund or replacement at no cost to you.

Safe delivery

If an item is lost in transit, NestKart will reship the item or issue a full refund including original shipping costs.

Damaged on arrival

Items damaged in shipping are eligible for free return and full refund or replacement. Report within 48 hours of delivery with photos to support@nestkart.com.

What data NestKart collects

  • Name, email address, shipping address, and phone number (used for delivery purposes)
  • Payment method details — processed entirely via Stripe; NestKart never stores card numbers
  • Order history and browsing behaviour on nestkart.com
  • Communication history with NestKart support

How NestKart uses your data

  • To fulfil and track your orders
  • To send order confirmation, dispatch, and delivery notification emails
  • To send marketing emails — only with your explicit opt-in; every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link
  • To improve the NestKart website, product range, and customer experience

How NestKart verifies reviews

  • Only verified purchasers can leave a product review — reviews require a confirmed delivery on record
  • Reviews are submitted after confirmed delivery; no pre-purchase reviews are permitted
  • NestKart does not edit or remove negative reviews unless they violate community guidelines (hate speech, spam, or off-topic content)
  • Brands cannot pay to suppress or remove reviews under any circumstances
  • Star ratings are calculated from all verified reviews with no weighting or manipulation
